## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an ultra-modern luxury creative engineering aesthetic. It projects precision, absolute computational authority, and avant-garde craftsmanship. Engineered for high-end studio applications, experimental tooling, and bespoke digital artifacts, the interface treats dark screen space as infinite physical void punctuated by surgical, energetic crimson emissions.

The design movement converges **Minimalism** with **High-Contrast Precision** and subtle **Tactile Glassmorphic Luminance**:
- **Void Depth:** Pure obsidian substrates (#000000 and #050507) prevent visual noise and grant components cinematic weight.
- **Controlled Radiance:** Neon ruby, cherry, and deep scarlet tones act as functional beacons and focal instruments rather than decorative paint.
- **Architectural Rigor:** Extreme typographic clarity, razor-sharp alignment grids, micro-etched borders, and atmospheric specular glows.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires technical mastery, exclusivity, focus, and quiet intensity.

## Colors

The palette operates under an uncompromising dark system where zero-luminance pitch black serves as the canvas, layered with carbon obsidian surfaces and animated by luminous crimson accents.

### Color Tokens & Semantic Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#000000`):** Pitch black foundation for viewport roots and extreme contrast zones.
- **Surface 01 - Obsidian Carbon (`#050507`):** Primary structural surface for views, workspaces, and navigation bars.
- **Surface 02 - Elevated Glass (`#0d0d12`):** Card frames, utility trays, and modal sheets.
- **Surface 03 - Micro-Tonal Panel (`#16161f`):** Active selections, segmented controls, and hover layers.
- **Stroke Subdued (`#262633`):** Subdued hairline structural divisions (1px).
- **Stroke Vibrant Accent (`#ff1e42`):** Active state indicators, focused states, and selected data rings.
- **Primary Accent - Cherry Neon (`#ff1e42`):** Primary interactions, dominant CTAs, key status signals, and energetic highlights.
- **Secondary Accent - Deep Crimson Glow (`#b91c1c`):** Accent outlines, gradient anchors, active indicator fills, and atmospheric backlights.
- **Tertiary Accent - Rose Ember (`#fda4af`):** Low-opacity tags, micro-badges, tertiary data series, and hover pings.
- **Accent Tints - Dark Wine Shadow (`#450a0a` / `#1c0407`):** Soft luminous backdrops, button depressed states, and card radial highlights.
- **Text Primary (`#f8fafc`):** Display headers, key values, primary interface labels.
- **Text Secondary (`#94a3b8`):** Narrative text, metadata descriptions, secondary labels.
- **Text Tertiary (`#475569`):** Disabled states, fine print, table column rules.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy relies on **Space Grotesk** across all primary touchpoints. Its geometric architecture, distinct idiosyncratic cuts, and tech-forward proportions supply the studio feel. A monospaced auxiliary face (**JetBrains Mono**) is reserved for raw telemetry, coordinates, hash stamps, and code blocks.

### Rules of Implementation
- **Letter Spacing:** Tighter letter spacing on large display levels (`-0.04em` to `-0.02em`) preserves typographic tension. Micro labels and technical badges receive positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) with optional uppercase styling for instant legibility.
- **Numbers & Data:** When rendering financial figures, timestamps, or system metrics, enable tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter.
- **Mobile Scale:** Viewport-dependent scaling downscales display and primary header tokens on viewports smaller than 768px via designated mobile tokens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a 12-column dynamic fluid grid anchored inside a maximum container width of `1440px`. Spacing intervals follow a strict geometric multiple derived from a 4px/8px baseline rhythm.

### Breakpoints & Grid Behavior
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `3rem` (48px) exterior page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) margins. Sidebars collapse to icon rails or off-canvas drawers.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) margins. Layout stacks vertically with horizontal scrolling chips or metric carousels.

### Layout Principles
- **Asymmetrical Tension:** Maintain clean structural margins while allowing primary cards or accent telemetry cards to anchor visual weight to the grid corners.
- **Dense Data Proximity:** Group metadata tightly (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) and separate distinct functional zones generously (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering combined with focused crimson photonic emission, rather than conventional drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Substrate (Pitch Black #000000):** Deepest level. Pure black backdrop of the application.
- **Base Layer (Obsidian #050507):** Main view containers and background canvases.
- **Elevated Level 1 (Obsidian Panel #0d0d12):** Standard card containers, table shells, and toolbars. Finished with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` or `rgba(255, 30, 66, 0.12)`.
- **Elevated Level 2 (Glass Surface #12121a):** Dropdowns, context menus, and tooltips. Incorporates `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Overlay Level 3 (Modal Surface #161622):** Dialogue windows and command palettes. Supported by a dark wine ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 50px -10px rgba(185, 28, 28, 0.25), 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

### Crimson Glow & Laser Accents
- Focused states and primary elements feature a controlled ruby aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(255, 30, 66, 0.35)`.
- Critical alert states or active indicators use micro-glows: `box-shadow: 0 0 8px #ff1e42`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts **Level 1 (Soft)** geometry. Radii are kept intentionally compact and precise, echoing milled metals, carbon hardware, and precision engineering gear.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Base Controls (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Checkboxes, micro badges, input fields, and tags.
- **Interactive Modules (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Standard buttons, segment switch buttons, and nested panels.
- **Containers & Cards (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Dashboard cards, data modules, modal dialogues, and preview viewports.
- **Pill Exceptions (`9999px`):** Reserved solely for live telemetry status dots, active radar indicators, and micro-counter pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Crimson:** Background `#ff1e42`, text `#ffffff`, font-weight 600, border radius 8px (`0.5rem`). Hover transition to `#ef4444` with ambient glow (`0 0 16px rgba(255, 30, 66, 0.4)`). Active state scales to `0.98` with `#b91c1c`.
- **Obsidian Secondary:** Background `#0d0d12`, 1px border of `#262633`, text `#f8fafc`. Hover border shifts to `rgba(255, 30, 66, 0.5)` with text shifting to `#ff1e42`.
- **Ghost / Minimal:** Background transparent, text `#94a3b8`. Hover background `rgba(255, 30, 66, 0.08)`, text `#f8fafc`.

### Cards & Panels
- **Structure:** Carbon obsidian fill (`#0d0d12`), bounded by a subtle 1px hairline border (`#1f1f2e`).
- **Interactive Hover:** Card border shifts to `rgba(255, 30, 66, 0.35)` with an ultra-subtle radial highlight centered on cursor position via wine gradient `radial-gradient(circle at var(--x) var(--y), rgba(185, 28, 28, 0.08) 0%, transparent 60%)`.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Dark inset background (`#050507`), 1px perimeter border of `#262633`, border radius 4px (`0.25rem`), typography Space Grotesk 14px.
- **Focus State:** 1px border in `#ff1e42` with an external halo `0 0 0 1px #ff1e42, 0 0 12px rgba(255, 30, 66, 0.25)`. Placeholder colored `#475569`.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- **Status Badges:** Solid dark backdrop (`rgba(255, 30, 66, 0.08)`), 1px border of `rgba(255, 30, 66, 0.2)`, text `#fda4af`, label-sm uppercase. Accompanied by a 6px neon ruby dot (`#ff1e42`) with subtle pulse animation.
- **Filter Chips:** Obsidian background (`#16161f`), 1px border (`#262633`). When active, fills with `#ff1e42` and text in `#ffffff`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** 16x16px square, radius 4px, border 1.5px `#333346` on `#050507`. When selected, fills with `#ff1e42` featuring a crisp white geometric checkmark and ruby aura.
- **Radio Buttons:** 16x16px circle, border 1.5px `#333346`. When checked, center is filled with an 8px solid `#ff1e42` pip surrounded by a 2px obsidian gap.

### Lists & Data Tables
- **Table Headers:** Border bottom 1px solid `#1f1f2e`, typography label-sm in `#475569`, tracking 0.08em uppercase.
- **Row Architecture:** Background transparent; hover reveals `#0d0d12` with a 2px left border accent in cherry red (`#ff1e42`). Row dividers use faint hairlines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`).

### Studio Metric Displays (Specialty Component)
- High-impact data readouts featuring huge display typography in `#f8fafc`, nested within an obsidian shell, flanked by mini sparklines colored in neon ruby `#ff1e42` and subtle dark-wine drop-shadows.
