## Brand & Style

This design system embodies warm architectural minimalism tailored for an ultra-high-end bespoke interior architecture and artisanal furniture studio. The visual ethos merges the tactile serenity of raw limestone, travertine, and smoked espresso timber with the precision of contemporary haute horlogerie and galleried architectural editorial design.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Architectural Serenity:** Vast, breathing expanses of warm negative space allow imagery of materiality, light, and form to command reverent attention.
- **Material Authenticity:** Surfaces evoke mineral limestone, honed stone, soft chalk, and patinated bronze rather than synthetic digital planes.
- **Tonal Subtlety:** Structure is defined through micro-tonal transitions, soft atmospheric blurs, and hairline mineral dividers rather than high-contrast structural containment.
- **Curated Exclusivity:** The experience feels akin to a private consultation in an atelier, deliberate, unhurried, tactile, and uncompromisingly refined.

## Colors

The color palette is derived from natural limestone quarries, aged French oak, cast bronze, and deep espresso carbon. It rejects stark digital whites and synthetic blacks in favor of deeply organic, light-absorbing pigments.

### Functional Palette Mapping
- **Primary (`#1C1917` - Deep Espresso Charcoal):** Grounded anchor tone applied to display typography, structural icons, focal buttons, and primary actions. It possesses the warmth of blackened ash wood.
- **Secondary (`#A27B5C` - Brushed Warm Bronze):** Sophisticated metallic warmth deployed for artisanal micro-accents, active pagination indicators, bespoke metadata, and delicate interactive feedback states.
- **Tertiary (`#44403C` - Raw Umber Stone):** Secondary editorial typography, captions, subtle structural outlines, and subdued iconography.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F7F5F0` - Honed Creamy Limestone):** The global canvas foundation. It emits a tactile, soft-touch stone ambiance that warms digital screens.
- **Surface Layering:**
  - *Base Canvas:* `#F7F5F0` (Limestone)
  - *Raised / Card Surface:* `#FDFCFA` (Pure Warm Alabaster)
  - *Muted Surface / Inset:* `#EDE8DF` (Poured Travertine)
  - *Subtle Border Hairline:* `rgba(68, 64, 60, 0.08)` (Micro Mineral Wash)

## Typography

The typography strategy leverages the balance between classical Roman serif proportions and precise architectural grotesque sans-serifs.

### Application Guidelines
- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Treated with quiet confidence. Use regular weights (`400` / `500`) with generous leading to prevent optical crowding. Display headlines should evoke high-fashion monographs and bespoke architectural folios. Avoid heavy bolds or italic overdrives; reserve italics solely for isolated single-word poetic emphasis.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its airy apertures, geometric legibility, and warm geometric geometry. Rendered primarily in light-regular weights (`300` to `400`) to let imagery lead visual density.
- **Labels & Metatags (`label-uppercase`):** Strictly set in all-caps with intentional letter spacing (`0.18em`). Used for material origins, spatial categories, dimensions, and index numbers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to an editorial, asymmetric 12-column fluid grid paired with generous horizontal and vertical breathing zones. 

### Rhythm & Proportions
- **Vertical Air:** Vertical gaps between major architectural suites and bespoke collection sections operate on a macro cadence (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`), eliminating cramped layouts in favor of meditative transitions.
- **Grid Adaptability:**
  - *Desktop (>1024px):* 12 columns, 64px margins, 48px gutters. Supports offset text-to-imagery pairings, hero lookbooks spanning 7-to-5 or 8-to-4 column splits, and full-bleed bleedouts with interior paddings.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1023px):* 8 columns, 32px margins, 24px gutters.
  - *Mobile (<767px):* 4 columns, 20px margins, 16px gutters. Structural blocks stack vertically with continuous vertical rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-elevation drop shadows and harsh drop lines, creating depth through light dispersion, tonal gradation, and architectural frosted translucency.

### Depth Conventions
- **Micro-Shadows (Diffuse Ambient Warmth):** Soft, low-contrast shadows tinted with warm charcoal (`rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)`) across an extended 32px to 48px blur radius. The effect replicates directional, late-afternoon sunlight washing across plaster.
- **Alabaster Glassmorphism:** Floating overlays (navigation bars, material swatch drawers, architectural popovers) utilize translucent limestone backing (`rgba(247, 245, 240, 0.78)`) combined with heavy hardware-accelerated backdrop blur (`24px`) and a delicate top-lit rim highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6) 1px inset`).
- **Surface Elevation Steps:**
  - *Level 0 (Earth):* Flat canvas `#F7F5F0`.
  - *Level 1 (Relief):* Plinth surfaces `#FDFCFA` elevated by a 1px border of `rgba(68, 64, 60, 0.06)` and ambient shadow `0 12px 36px -12px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)`.
  - *Level 2 (Float / Overlay):* Modals and dropdowns elevated with `0 24px 64px -16px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, stone-carved architectural sensibility with subtle corner softenings (`roundedness: 1`).

### Shape Philosophy
- **Soft Milled Edges:** Rather than stark 0px industrial cuts or playful bubble radiuses, components emulate master-crafted stonework and hand-sanded timber. Standard elements carry a 4px (`0.25rem`) radius, while cards and surface modules carry an 8px (`0.5rem`) soft bevel.
- **Full Radius Badging:** Category pills, status beacons, and circular material selectors employ full pill rounding (`9999px`) to contrast cleanly against geometric furniture layouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Espresso Solid):** `#1C1917` background with `#F7F5F0` text, `4px` corner radius, `12px 28px` padding. Monospaced or uppercase sans-serif label (`label-uppercase`). Hover subtly elevates surface tint to `#2E2A27` alongside a gentle `translateY(-1px)`.
- **Secondary Action (Limestone Outline):** Transparent background, `1px` border in `rgba(68, 64, 60, 0.25)`, text `#1C1917`. On hover, fills with `rgba(68, 64, 60, 0.04)` and border transitions to `#A27B5C`.
- **Text Link / Bespoke Action:** Text-only, `#1C1917` with an animated hairline underline (`#A27B5C`) that expands smoothly from center on hover.

### Product & Portfolio Cards
- Constructed with `#FDFCFA` background and minimal structure. Images feature fixed architectural aspect ratios (`4:5` portrait or `16:10` wide panoramic).
- Overlaid tags (e.g., "Carrara Marble & Aged Teak") sit at the bottom-left in soft glass plaques (`rgba(247, 245, 240, 0.85)`).
- Card titles use `headline-sm` with subtext and dimensional specs rendered in `label-uppercase` bronze (`#A27B5C`).

### Material Swatches & Chips
- Circular and rounded micro-rectangles encased in hairline rims. Selected states display an inner concentric bronze ring (`#A27B5C`) separated by a `2px` creamy gap.
- Filter chips feature transparent backing with `1px` tinted borders; active filters switch to solid `#1C1917` with `#F7F5F0` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Minimalist open or bottom-bordered designs: `0px` side/top borders, `1px` solid border bottom (`rgba(68, 64, 60, 0.2)`), `#F7F5F0` canvas integration.
- Focus transitions bottom border to `#A27B5C` accompanied by a floating label in `label-uppercase`.

### Architectural Curated Lists & Specifications
- Horizontal line dividers rendered in ultra-delicate `rgba(68, 64, 60, 0.08)`.
- Layout pairs spec parameters (e.g., "Joinery", "Origin", "Finish") on the left in `body-sm` with artisanal definitions on the right in `body-md` deep charcoal.
